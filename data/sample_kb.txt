# Artificial Intelligence and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is an AI architecture that enhances large language models (LLMs) by retrieving relevant information from an external knowledge base before generating a response.

## Core Components of Hybrid RAG
1. Dense Retrieval: Uses dense embedding models such as sentence-transformers/all-minilm-l6-v2 to perform semantic vector search in a vector database like Qdrant.
2. Sparse Retrieval: Uses BM25 to find exact keyword matches, proper names, and domain-specific terminology.
3. Rank Fusion: Combines dense semantic rankings and sparse lexical rankings using Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF).
4. Cross-Encoder Reranking: Uses cross-encoders such as BAAI/bge-reranker-v2-m3 to score query-document pairs simultaneously for maximum precision.

## Multi-Hop Question Answering
Multi-hop reasoning requires synthesizing facts from two or more distinct passages to answer a single question. For example, determining who the founder of an organization is and what year their birthplace was established.
